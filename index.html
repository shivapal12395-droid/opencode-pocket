OpenCodePocket

Your coding agent, in your pocket.

How do I get these?

In Termux, start the server:

opencode serve --hostname 127.0.0.1 --port 4096

It prints a server password on start. Or create a one-time link:

opencode pair

Paste either the password or the whole pairing link above. The link expires in 5 minutes.

The app talks straight to your phone. Nothing leaves your network unless you expose it yourself.
